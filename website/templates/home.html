About the book

Better data sharing,
by design.

Data sharing creates possibilities. It also brings responsibilities.

Data Sharing with GDPR explores how to balance legal compliance, ethical responsibility, and technical interoperability when working with personal data.

From the foundations of GDPR to secure system design and real-world case studies, the book connects the principles with the decisions that put them into practice.
