Ensures data is processed lawfully, fairly and transparently
Article 5.1.b
Purpose Limitation

Only data necessary for the purpose should be processed
Article 5.1.d
Accuracy
Personal data must be accurate and kept up to date
Article 5.1.e
Storage Limitation
Keep data no longer than necessary
Article 5.1.f
Integrity and Confidentiality
Ensure security and confidentiality of personal data

Accountability
Controllers are responsible for GDPR compliance
