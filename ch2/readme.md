# Chapter 2: Foundations of GDPR

## Overview
This directory contains the resources and supplementary materials for **Chapter 2: Foundations of GDPR**.

## Contents

### `data_analysis`
Contains data analysis resources related to GDPR enforcement actions, including statistics and insights on GDPR penalties and fines.

### Interactive guides
The GDPR framework guide, the GDPR articles reference, and the principles mindmap are published on the companion website. Their content lives in [`website/content/pages/`](../website/content/pages/) and the mindmap image in [`website/static/ch2/`](../website/static/ch2/).
