About This Book

This comprehensive guide explores the intersection of data sharing and the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). From foundational concepts to practical implementation, this book provides essential insights for organizations, researchers, and practitioners navigating the complex landscape of privacy-preserving data sharing.

Whether you're building data sharing systems, ensuring GDPR compliance, or understanding the ethical implications of data processing, this book serves as your authoritative reference for modern data protection challenges.
