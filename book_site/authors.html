Dr. Tek Raj Chhetri
Postdoctoral Associate
Massachusetts Institute of Technology, USA
Center for Artificial Intelligence (AI) Research Nepal
Dr. Tek Raj Chhetri is the founder and director of the Center for Artificial Intelligence (AI) Research Nepal (CAIR-Nepal) and a postdoctoral associate at the Massachusetts Institute of Technology, USA. His scholarly pursuits revolve around the intricate realms of data privacy and AI. His research on AI concentrates on AI explainability and the sculpting of data privacy and Internet of Things (IoT) data sharing via the intricate design of scalable, interoperable, and GDPR-compliant systems.
During his doctoral studies, he contributed to developing a scalable GDPR-compliance verification tool, which was part of his doctoral thesis graded as excellent, and has been recognized as one of the key deep tech innovations by the European Innovation Radar. His work on GDPR has also impacted the policy document "Advancing accountability in AI" by the OECD (Organisation for Economic Co-operation and Development) and Federal Ministry of Labour and Social Affairs Germany with the support of around 200 experts.
In addition, Tek Raj Chhetri has experience with software development, cloud computing, and autonomous vehicles for industrial and research purposes. He has (co-) authored over 20 referred publications in top journals and conferences in the areas of artificial intelligence, data sharing and GDPR.
https://tekrajchhetri.com
Areas of Expertise:
Artificial Intelligence Data Privacy Knowledge Graphs Responsible AI Cloud Computing Autonomous Vehicles
Dr. Semih Yumusak
Research Fellow
University of Southampton, UK
Dr. Semih Yumusak is currently a Research Fellow at the Web and Internet Science (WAIS) group, which researches large-scale systems that combine computational, data, and human components at the University of Southampton. Prior to joining the University of Southampton, Dr. Yumusak was the head of the department at the KTO Karatay University Turkey and a lecturer at Koc University, one of the top universities in Turkey, where he lectured a master's level course on Big Data Systems.
Dr. Yumusak holds an MBA degree in addition to his doctoral degree in Computer Engineering. Dr. Yumusak has worked (or is working) on several European projects, such as (i) QROWD, a project that focuses on cross-sectorial big data stream integration to improve city services like mobility and quality of life of citizens; (ii) UPCAST, project that focuses on developing GDPR-compliant plugins for data sharing and monetization of data; and (iii) RAISE, a project that focuses on enabling FAIR data sharing and processing in a privacy-aware manner.
In addition to his academic experience, Dr. Yumusak has over 15 years of industrial experience, both developing and managing large scale cloud and network systems. Further to working on cloud and network systems, Dr. Yumusak has also worked in the area of artificial intelligence developing predictive models with AI4BD AG. He has (co-) authored over 20 refereed publications.
Areas of Expertise:
Big Data Systems Cloud Computing Network Systems Predictive AI Models FAIR Data Sharing GDPR Compliance
Dr. Luis-Daniel Ibáñez
Associate Professor

Dr. Luis-Daniel Ibáñez is a Lecturer at the University of Southampton where he leads the MSc. Data Science program. Prof. Dr. Anna Fensel
Professor
Wageningen University, the Netherlands
Prof. Dr. Anna Fensel (former name: Anna V. Zhdanova) is a Full Professor in Artificial Intelligence and Data Science at Wageningen University & Research, Wageningen, the Netherlands. Previously, she was an Associate Professor and Senior Assistant Professor at STI Innsbruck, Department of Computer Science, University of Innsbruck, Austria, and prior to this worked as a Senior Researcher at FTW – Telecommunications Research Center Vienna, Austria, and a Research Fellow at the University of Surrey, UK, and as a project employee at DERI Innsbruck, University of Innsbruck, Austria.
Anna has earned both her habilitation (2018) and her doctoral degree (2006) in Computer Science at the University of Innsbruck. Prior to that, she has received a diploma in Mathematics and Computer Science equivalent to the Master degree in 2003 from Novosibirsk State University, Russia.
Anna has been extensively involved in European and national projects related to Semantic technologies, e.g. as a consortium coordinator (EURegio project REEeT, FFG projects TourPack, SESAME-S and SESAME, ÖAD project RESIDE), a partner project manager (H2020 OntoCommons, H2020 smashHit, Eurostars WordLiftNG, Interreg KI-Net, BMWi-FFG CampaNeo, H2020 ENTROPY, FFG DALICC, FFG OpenFridge, FP7 CSA BYTE, FP7 CA BIG, FP7 CA PRELIDA, FP7 STREP m:Ciudad, FP7 NoE PlanetData, AAL PeerAssist, FP6 IST IP SPICE, FP5 IST project Esperonto) as well as a technical contributor in other numerous projects.
She has been a co-organizer or a Program Committee member of more than 100 scientific events including being in chair roles at top events such as SEMANTiCS and ESWC, a reviewer for numerous journals and a project proposals evaluator for funding agencies (EU H2020 and FP6, Eureka-Eurostars, national funding). She is a (co-)author of ca. 150 refereed publications.

Artificial Intelligence Data Science Semantic Technologies European Projects Research Leadership Academic Excellence
Prof. George Konstantinidis
Professor
2 University of Southampton, UK
Prof. George Konstantinidis is a Professor at the School of Electronics and Computer Science, University of Southampton. His research interests include data management and A.I., data integration, data privacy, the semantic web, and distributed systems. He has been involved in a number of US, EU and UK projects, and has published over 40 articles in international conferences and journals.
He is currently the scientific coordinator for the Horizon Europe project UPCAST that is delivering a set of universal tools for data marketplaces.
